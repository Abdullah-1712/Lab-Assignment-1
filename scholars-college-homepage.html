Welcome to The Scholars Science College

The Scholars Science College is a highly developed education network in Wah Cantt, dedicated to academic excellence and character building. Our students are members of an illustrious institution proud of a heritage built on cultural, demographic, and economic diversity.

TSSC works in partnership with community, families, and students to ensure that every student acquires the knowledge, skills, and core values necessary to achieve personal success and grow into a responsible citizen of a global society. The college has achieved outstanding Federal Board positions over the years.

Our Vision

To enable each student to flourish as a responsible citizen of the global community.

Our Mission

To provide quality education through gifted and talented programs that nurture creativity, critical thinking, and academic potential.

Admission Steps

  1. Visit the campus or official website for the prospectus.
  2. Fill out the admission form completely.
  3. Attach required documents (certificates, CNIC/B-Form, photos).
  4. Submit the form along with the admission fee.
  5. Appear for the entry test / interview (if applicable).
  6. Check the merit list on the notice board or website.
  7. Complete enrollment and fee submission.
